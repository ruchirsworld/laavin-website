Commercial & Campus Landscaping

Home Services Landscaping Services

Turnkey Landscaping Solutions for Commercial & Civic Spaces

We transform outdoor environments into stunning, functional, and eco-friendly landscapes for businesses, educational campuses, government facilities, and developers. With over two decades of experience in institutional design and execution, we offer tailored solutions that align with your sustainability and corporate identity goals.

1. Landscape Architecture & Master Planning

Combining artistic vision with site engineering to craft sustainable master plans for corporate headquarters, universities, and public parks. Our designs prioritize indigenous plant selections, sun-path analysis, and rainwater harvesting integration.

2. Professional Plant Installation & Micro-Climates

From massive shade trees and specimen ornamentals to micro-climate ground covers, we procure and install healthy nursery stock with specialized soil remediation to ensure rapid rooting and disease resistance.

3. Terrace & Rooftop Gardens

Turn underutilized building roofs into lush, functional terrace gardens. We engineer lightweight growing media, high-performance waterproof drainage membranes, and drought-hardy flora to enhance building insulation and employee wellbeing.

4. Vertical Gardens & Living Green Walls

State-of-the-art living walls for interior corporate lobbies and exterior facades. Our modular vertical systems feature integrated automated drip nutrition and air purification benefits.

5. Sports Turfing & Grounds Construction

Professional construction and turfing of international-standard cricket pitches, football grounds, golf greens, and athletics tracks with precision laser-leveling and multi-layer subsoil drainage.

6. Landscape Project Management & Execution

End-to-end site oversight, contractor coordination, timeline control, and quality audits ensuring turnkey delivery within budget for complex multi-crore infrastructure developments.

7. Comprehensive Landscape Facility Maintenance

Dedicated on-site horticultural maintenance teams providing scheduled pruning, seasonal aeration, organic fertilization, and IPM (Integrated Pest Management) to keep campuses pristine year-round.
