Hardscaping services

Built elements that last as long as the landscape.

Paths, water features, lighting and shade structures for hotels, offices and farmhouses, built for heavy footfall, harsh summers and the monsoon.

Book a Site Visit ↗

What we deliver

Six hardscape elements, engineered to last.

Our hardscape work is overseen by a civil and electrical engineer with 30+ years at the Delhi Development Authority, so structure, drainage and wiring are right before the finishes go on.

01

Water features and fountains

Fountains, cascades and reflecting pools with efficient recirculating pumps and underwater lighting, designed to be easy to clean and maintain.

02

Pathways and paving

Walkways, courtyards and driveways in natural stone, interlocking pavers and stamped concrete. Permeable pavers let rainwater soak in, and accessible routes are built in where needed.

03

Landscape lighting

Energy-efficient LED bollards, tree uplighting, underwater lights and solar lights that make gardens safe and attractive after dark.

04

Fencing and metalwork

MS and SS railings, decorative screens and boundary fencing, finished to resist rust and weather.

05

Pergolas, gazebos and shade structures

Timber, aluminium and tensile structures for seating areas, poolsides, terraces and breakout spaces, designed for shade and airflow.

06

Sculptures and feature walls

Stone and metal sculptures, murals and feature walls that give a hotel, office or farmhouse a recognisable focal point.

Have drawings or a layout ready?

Send them over. Our engineer will review the hardscape scope and prepare a detailed, itemised estimate.

How we work

Four steps from first visit to a garden that lasts.

Book a Site Visit ↗
  1. 01

    Site visit

    We visit, test the soil, check sun, water and drainage, and listen to what you need.

  2. 02

    Design and estimate

    Drawings, a plant list and a clear itemised estimate, so you know the cost before work starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    Our crews plant, build and install irrigation, with one project manager keeping you updated.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    Scheduled care after handover, monthly photo reports, and failed plants replaced on written terms.
