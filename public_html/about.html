Our story

From EGW Services to Laavin Infrascapes.

We started as EGW Services, a landscaping and horticulture firm. As the work grew to highway corridors, industrial green belts and landfill slopes, we became Laavin Infrascapes. Those projects, including tree transplantation on NH-307 and a green belt at the Delhi Airport fuel facility, taught us to plan for survival, keep paperwork in order and finish when promised.

Today we bring that discipline to terrace gardens, hotels, office premises and farmhouses, where a landscape has to look good every day, not just on handover.

How we work differently

Most landscaping fails after the contractor leaves. Ours is planned to last.

We plan for survival

Species are chosen for the site's soil, sun, wind and water, and aftercare is part of the scope, not an extra.

We plan the water first

Drip and sprinkler irrigation is designed with the planting, so gardens stay green on less water and less labour.

We engineer what is underneath

Waterproofing, drainage and load on terraces, and paving and wiring on the ground, are checked by an engineer before planting starts.

We stay after handover

Scheduled maintenance, monthly photo reports and written replacement terms keep the landscape you paid for alive next year and the year after.
