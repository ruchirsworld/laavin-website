Mrs. Alka Jain

Mrs. Alka Jain is a seasoned horticulturist with a Master of Science degree in Botany. With over 30 years of hands-on experience, she is renowned for her deep understanding of plant biology and a keen eye for sustainable landscaping solutions. Mrs. Jain's expertise lies in creating harmonious environments that blend seamlessly with nature, enhancing the well-being of communities and the ecosystem

Mr. Ramesh Chandra Pathak

Mr. Ramesh Chandra Pathak is our seasoned engineer with over 30 years of experience in civil and electrical engineering. As a retiree from the DDA, he has been instrumental in shaping the urban landscape of Delhi. His expertise ensures that our projects are not only visually stunning but also structurally sound and energy-efficient, reflecting his long-standing commitment to quality.

Mr. Ruchir Jain

Mr. Ruchir Jain is our talented Landscape Designer, known for their innovative designs and attention to detail. With a keen eye for aesthetics and a deep understanding of horticulture. He creates landscapes that are both beautiful and functional.

Mr. Shashank Pathak

Mr. Shashank Pathak is our dedicated and organized Project Manager. With a focus on customer satisfaction and project efficiency. He ensures that our projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest standards.
