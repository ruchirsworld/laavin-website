About us

Plant science meets solid engineering.

Laavin Infrascapes is a Delhi landscaping and horticulture company. We design, build and maintain gardens, green belts and irrigation for offices, hotels, builders, societies and homes across Delhi NCR, North India and Central India.

Our work grew on demanding sites: highway corridors, industrial green belts and landfill slopes, for clients such as NHAI, Indian Railways and the Delhi Airport fuel facility. Those projects taught us to plan for survival, keep our paperwork in order and finish when promised. Today we bring the same discipline to every terrace, hotel, office and farmhouse.

Questions

Answers to common questions.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in New Ashok Nagar, Delhi. We work across Delhi NCR, North India and Central India. For example, we moved 80 trees for Indian Railways in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh.

Can you move a big tree instead of cutting it down?

Yes. We check the tree, prepare its roots, lift it with a crane and replant it. Then we water and check it until it grows new roots. On NH-307 we moved 155 mature trees for an NHAI project.

Do you look after the garden after it is built?

Yes. We offer regular maintenance. You get a photo report every month, and we replace plants that fail on terms agreed in writing.

Can my terrace hold a garden?

Often, yes, but we check first. Our engineer checks the roof load and waterproofing before any work starts. We use light soil and drip watering to keep the weight and water low.

Do you work with facility management (FM) companies?

Yes. You can hire us directly, or we can work as the garden specialist under your FM company contract.

How do I get a price?

Book a site visit, or send photos of your site on WhatsApp to (+91) 98115 11558. We reply within 24 hours. After the site visit, you get drawings and an item-wise estimate.
