Irrigation and horticulture

Irrigation and horticulture for sites of every size.

From drip lines on a farmhouse lawn to moving mature trees on a national highway, our horticulture team plans the living part of every project.

Book a Site Visit ↗

What we deliver

Five services for the living part of your site.

A botanist with over 30 years of field experience leads our horticulture team. We plan the plants, soil treatment and watering for each site, and keep checking after handover.

01

Drip and sprinkler irrigation

Automatic drip and sprinkler systems that water each plant when it needs it. They save water and labour on terraces, gardens, farmhouses and long highway stretches. We design, install and service the system.

02

Mature tree transplantation

When building work puts mature trees in the way, we move them instead of cutting them down. We check and prune each tree, dig and protect the root ball, lift and carry it, and replant it. Then we stay on to water, feed and check it.

Delivered: We moved 155 mature roadside trees for an NHAI road-widening project on NH-307.
03

Highway median and avenue plantation

Hardy shrubs and trees that cope with pollution, for road medians, roadsides and long drives. A well-planted median cuts headlight glare and handles dust, heat and little water.

Delivered: We planted 3,500 plants on the NH-7 median for an NHAI contractor.
04

Industrial green belts

Thick belts of native trees around factories, fuel depots and public sites, as environmental clearances often require. We pick trees that trap dust, survive the site and follow its safety rules.

Delivered: We built a green belt and landscape that meets the safety rules at the Delhi Airport fuel facility.
05

Erosion control and slope turfing

Grass and plants that hold slopes, embankments and covered landfills in place through the monsoon.

Delivered: We laid turf over 2.5 acres of the Gazipur landfill in Delhi to hold the slope.

How we work

Four steps from first visit to a garden that lasts.

Book a Site Visit ↗
  1. 01

    Site visit

    We visit your site, test the soil and check sunlight, water and drainage. We listen to what you need.

  2. 02

    Design and estimate

    You get drawings, a plant list and an item-wise estimate. You know the cost before we start.

  3. 03

    Build

    Our team plants, builds and installs the irrigation. One project manager keeps you updated.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    We look after the garden after handover, send a photo report every month and replace failed plants on written terms.
