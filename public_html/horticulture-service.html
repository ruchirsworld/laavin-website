Horticultural services

Trees and planting for sites of every size.

From mature tree transplantation on a national highway to green belts around industrial sites, our horticulture team plans the living part of every project.

Book a Site Visit ↗

What we deliver

Four services for the living part of your site.

Our horticulture team is led by a botanist with 30+ years of field experience. Species, soil treatment and watering are planned for each site.

01

Mature tree transplantation

When construction puts mature trees in the way, we move them instead of felling them. We survey and prune each tree, dig and protect the root ball, lift and transport it, replant it at the new site, and stay on for watering and feeding.

Delivered: On NH-307 we relocated 155 mature roadside trees for an NHAI widening project.

02

Highway medians and avenues

Hardy, pollution-tolerant shrubs and trees for medians, roadsides and long driveways. A well-planted median cuts headlight glare and copes with dust, heat and limited watering.

Delivered: We planted 3,500 plants on the NH-7 median for an NHAI contractor.

03

Green belts and slope turfing

Industrial green belts

Dense native green buffers around factories, fuel facilities and civic infrastructure, as environmental clearances often require. Species are chosen to trap dust, survive the site and respect its safety rules.

Delivered: At the Delhi Airport fuel facility we built a safety-compliant green belt and landscape.

Erosion control and slope turfing

Turf and vegetation that hold slopes, embankments and capped landfills in place through the monsoon.

Delivered: We laid bio-turfing over 2.5 acres of the Gazipur landfill in Delhi for slope stabilisation.
